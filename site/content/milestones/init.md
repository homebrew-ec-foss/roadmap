# What was the idea behind Homebrew?

Homebrew is a gathering place for FOSS enthusiasts on campus. It was created with the idea to solve the absence of a major FOSS Community in PES. It serves as a hub where enthusiasts engage in conversations about their preferred open source technologies, share alternatives, and propose different software options.

## But what is FOSS?
In contrast to proprietary software, Free and Open Source Software or FOSS is open for all to study, use, modify and redistribute under an open-source license. A good example is [anna](https://anna-docs.netlify.app/), a lightning-fast static site generator developed by our members that powers this very website! 
To learn more about FOSS, check out [Homebrew](https://homebrew.hsp-ec.xyz/) - our very own FOSS wing that's open to all open-source enthusiasts! 

## What We Do
- Firesides 🔥 Fireside chats where community members share insights, lessons learned, and real-world experiences on FOSS, technology, and innovation.

- Workshops — Hands-on sessions and technical talks covering everything from Git workflows to advanced FOSS concepts

- FOSS Talks — A curated series where speakers dive deep into open-source projects and community-driven development.

- Homebrew — Our FOSS Wing, where members contribute to open-source projects

- Tilde - our mentorship program over the summer break 

Homebrew has established itself as a valuable platform for sharing thoughts, ideas, and guides related to emerging technologies. Members of the community contribute their insights to assist and guide other individuals seeking information.
