# What is Tilde ?
Tilde is essentially a mentorship program, the mentees selected would be working under the guidance of the project leads, where all the decisions are taken by the mentees. The mentor's job is to point them in the right direction and help them understand what they are working with.

## How does Tilde work ?
Once the mentors of the projects have finalised their teams, given the short duration, we first focus on onboarding the team as quick as possible, get them familiar with what they are going to be working with. Mentors would be providing all the mentees with all sorts of resources to help them out. The subsequent weeks will be focused on working on the actual project. This is sort of a general timeline, but it can be completely different depending on the project. (For example, work on the actual project could begin as soon as the first day after onboarding given that the team are familiar with their tools, and some could take 1-2 weeks to just get introduced and get familiar with what they are working with)

## What domins do Tilde Mentees work on ?
Tilde Project topics are abstract we try to include most of the domains possible , if a mentor is ready to take up a domian and mentor the mentees then we are going to have that domian 
Some of the popular Domains are 
- Systems and Low Level programming
- Compiler Design 
- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
- Electronics 
**And many more**

## How does it continue ?
While Tilde only goes on for 5 weeks, we encourage the teams to keep working on their projects. After the 5 weeks mentees would be in a position to take complete ownership of their projects the repositry permissions would be changed and the mentees can take the project forward in whichever direction they want.

## Past iterations of Tilde

### Tilde 3.0

- **[psh](https://homebrew.hsp-ec.xyz/posts/tilde-3.0-psh/)** — A fancy POSIX-like shell built from the ground up.
- **[ModelForge](https://homebrew.hsp-ec.xyz/posts/tilde-3.0-modelforge/)** — A low-code machine learning platform for dataset preparation and model development.
- **[Bal.rs](https://homebrew.hsp-ec.xyz/posts/tilde-3.0-bal.rs/)** — A simple load balancer written in Rust.

### Tilde 4.0

- **[CHIP-Monks](https://homebrew.hsp-ec.xyz/posts/tilde-4.0-chip-monks:-hardware-implementation-of-chip-8/)** — A hardware implementation of CHIP-8 using Verilog, capable of simulating games such as Pong and Tetris.
- **[Prob.lm](https://homebrew.hsp-ec.xyz/posts/tilde-4.0-prob.lm/)** — A study assistant built using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
- **[JamSesh](https://homebrew.hsp-ec.xyz/posts/tilde-4.0-jamsesh:-multi-device-audio-playback-with-webrtc/)** — A multi-device audio playback system using WebRTC.
- **[Eventloop](https://homebrew.hsp-ec.xyz/posts/tilde-4.0-eventloop/)** — An event management system for managing events and hackathons.


### Tilde 5.0

Tilde 5.0 featured projects spanning systems, compilers, machine learning, networking, and hardware:

- **[muSSHroom](https://homebrew.hsp-ec.xyz/posts/tilde-5.0-musshroom:-terminal-native-ssh-chat-server/)** — An SSH-chat inspired server with rooms and Markdown support.
- **[Ziguana](https://homebrew.hsp-ec.xyz/posts/tilde-5.0-ziguana:a-transpiler-built-in-zig/)** — A Zig-based transpiler for a lightweight custom scripting language that compiles to C.
- **[Barrel-ly Learning](https://homebrew.hsp-ec.xyz/posts/tilde-5.0-barrel-ly-learning-creating-a-model-to-play-donkey-kong-using-imitation-learning-and-actor-critic-reinforcement-learning./)** — A reinforcement learning neural network trained to play Donkey Kong using imitation learning and actor-critic reinforcement learning.
- **[RepoLens](https://homebrew.hsp-ec.xyz/posts/tilde-5.0-repolens:an-efficient-retrieval-engine-focused-on-codebases/)** — An efficient retrieval engine focused on codebases.
- **NetStacc** — A project focused on building a DIY TCP/IP stack.
- **Sillycon** — An exploration of silicon artwork through synthesizable RTL, simulation, and open-source hardware tools.
- **Graphiti** — A project exploring meaningful patterns in graphs using Neural Graph Pattern Machines and applying them to graph-based tasks.
- **Twix** — A self-supervised learning model that identifies items using the Barlow Twins method.
